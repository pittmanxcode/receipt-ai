receipt.ai

A record of corrections, and what gets built on it.

21 months of correcting an AI, written down. Every rule that came out of it carries its receipt — a dated correction, or a labeled source. Five of them have no external twin. The record is the point; the app is what the record is for.

One app, four parts

Building

The pieces already run separately. This puts them behind one door, so a note captured in the morning is filed, checked, and scheduled without being re-entered anywhere.

  • Rules The working ruleset, each rule carrying its correction or its source.
  • Audit Re-checks its own filing on a schedule, logs every move and why.
  • OS The daily loop: capture, file, brief, review, act.
  • Notes aggregation Notes from every source land in one inbox instead of four.
