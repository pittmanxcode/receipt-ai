# How I Make AI Work for Me — Taxonomy v1 (receipt.ai personal layer)

Built bottom-up from a 21-month correction record (Mar 2025 – Aug 2026): date-sampled sweeps of ~100 chats plus correction-shaped probes across the full history. Six categories. Product-limitation items are excluded by decision and live in the build backlog. Status marks: resolved / recurring / installed-unproven.

## Categories

1. **Fidelity to what I said** — exact words, exact facts, exact method, exact ask-shape. Status: RECURRING (Mar 2025 – Aug 2026, incl. "commercial not enterprise" Aug 21 2026 and a $200K-total vs $100K/yr parameter misread Jun 29 2026).
2. **Evidence discipline** — labeled numbers, unknown-over-guess, no invented figures or distinctions. Status: RESOLVED WHEN BRIEFED, RECURRING WITHOUT THE BRIEF (standard originates Oct 23 2025; unlabeled-average pause Aug 2026).
3. **Scope discipline** — nothing added, nothing steered, nothing touched beyond the ask. Status: RECURRING (May 2026 cluster; seeded-search correction Aug 2026).
4. **Format and economy** — length control, summary-first, format matched to use (manual for doing, prose for reading/listening, inline over file). Status: RECURRING — longest-running unfixed pattern (four dated "shorter" corrections Mar 2025 – Jun 2026; inline-prose rewrite asked five times through Aug 29 2026).
5. **Mode matching** — teaching / listening / listing / verdict each have a shape. Status: RESOLVED BY RULE (each miss corrected once, no logged recurrence after Jun 2025).
6. **Process control** — checkpoints, correction audits, goal audits, task splitting, delivery verification. Status: INSTALLED AUG 2026, UNPROVEN (thread-splitting practice self-invented by Apr 2026).

## Correction ledger (the receipts)

**A. Length and economy** — "Is there any way to make it shorter?" (Mar 17 2025). "If it's too long they're probably not going to read the whole thing" (Apr 2 2025). "Wayyy too long — we need it about half this size" (May 15 2025). Shorter, informal, pasteable over a long document (Jun 24 2026).

**B. Ask-shape misreads** — asked for a list of 100, got a prose sampler: "I think you mistook what I was asking for" (Jun 27 2025). Pointed at a root problem, got surface tactics: "I don't just want coping mechanisms — there is an underlying problem" (Mar 19 2025). Over-prescribing and solution-jumping before the picture was complete (Apr 23 2026).

**C. Additions and steering** — a clarifying question angled to move the decision ("ik why your question is trying to move it somewhere else"), an invented distinction ("feels like random ai slop"), a tactic contradicting the stated model (cut), and an admission of "adding jobs" — all May 23 2026.

**D. Reframes that shrink reality** — "just make music" rejected as wishful thinking, not a plan (May 22 2026); projected motivations corrected (Apr 23 2026); skeptical analysis explicitly requested over encouragement (May 2026).

**E. Precision on my facts** — "COMMERCIAL B2B — not enterprise. Small vocabulary tweak with massive downstream impact" (Aug 21 2026). Program-lead comp misread, $200K total vs $100K/yr minimum, plus inflated cost assumptions pushed down (Jun 29 2026).

**F. Delivery integrity** — "I never got the updated versions though": files claimed delivered, never received (Feb 27 2026). The preemptive guard now written in caps: "DO NOT MAKE CHANGES — just read them and flag" (Apr 10 2026).

**H. Standards pre-installed (the going-well side)** — leave blank what isn't certain, offer "need more info," "always remind me" (Oct 23 2025); skeptical-over-encouragement (May 2026); the DO-NOT-CHANGE guard (Apr 2026). Corrections converted into prompt armor since at least Oct 2025.

**One-off kept visible, unrolled:** the undelivered-files incident (single occurrence).

## Caveats layer

Going well: corrections become standards that hold (Oct 2025 unknown-over-guess became the written research brief); mode misses die after one correction; context-splitting was self-invented four months before it was named as a rule. Not going well: length survived 15 months of corrections; fidelity errors still land with armor installed; one delivery failure cost enough trust to produce an all-caps guard. Open honesty item: the record cannot say whether 2026's correction density means worse behavior or heavier scope — both moved at once, and pre-March-2025 evidence is too thin to baseline.
