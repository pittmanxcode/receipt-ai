# receipt.ai — Eval Set v1 (public, obfuscated)

Ten real asks with their ground truth: what a correct answer had to contain, not just what it had to sound like. Each one is drawn from an actual chat, listed with the source file or conversation it came from and roughly when.

This is the obfuscated copy. Personal specifics are generalized — exact dollar figures become ranges, a specific vehicle becomes "an older hybrid," references to particular people become role-generic. Public context that makes the goal real is kept as-is: cities, genres, event types. The input lines are verbatim. The full-detail version is private and is not committed to this repository.

1. **Housing** — Input: "im done having roommates, how do i find a place to live by myself thats actually affordable" — Ground truth: solo living space, defined as no daily interaction with housemates; under roughly $1,000–1,200/month all-in; open on location and housing type at this stage; wants a survey of current availability before filtering. Source: housing-search file, Aug 2026.

2. **Nevada promoters** — Input: "my friends a hard tekk dj trying to get booked in vegas or reno, who do we even reach out to" — Ground truth: map of Reno/Las Vegas hard dance promoters likely to book a hard tekk artist (another artist's profile, not the requester's own); built bottom-up with no seeding from named promoters; big national brands deprioritized, local independents favored; prioritized by past lineups, artist levels booked, and venue-capacity signals; includes underground/renegade crews; delivered as a spreadsheet with links, verification kept manual on the requester's side. Source: bookings file, Aug 2026.

3. **Venue call sheet** — Input: "whats a cheap venue in oakland where i could throw a party for like 500 people" — Ground truth: a call sheet of low-cost venues for a ticketed public event with amplified music; four hard filters — low rent (hourly, minimum-spend, or revenue split; no flat day-rate halls), 400–700 capacity, zoned or permittable, dry or outside-bar-OK so a caterer split survives; swept zone by zone (Oakland core, Oakland/San Leandro border, West Berkeley/Emeryville, Alameda); output as an xlsx with live-call columns; breadth enforced over editorial judgment. Source: "Finding low-cost Venues" chat, May 2026.

4. **Venue funding** — Input: "theres this warehouse venue that needs fire safety upgrades, is there grant money or something for that" — Ground truth: funding routes for a specific Oakland venue's fire/life-safety compliance — grant strategies plus fiscal sponsorship via Intersection for the Arts — packaged as a partnership pitch trading grant research for event access. Source: venue-funding file.

5. **Radiator cap** — Input: "lost my radiator cap, do i need the exact one for my car or will any cap work" — Ground truth: same-day replacement for a lost cap on an older hybrid after the nearest yard had no exact match; needs the interchange logic (pressure rating and neck design over make/model), the fact that these hybrids run two reservoir caps so which one is missing must be confirmed, and the yard-inventory-vs-buy-new routes. Source: "Finding a replacement radiator cap" chat, Aug 2026.

6. **Job evaluation** — Input: "im in tech sales and want to switch companies, which ones are actually good to sell for right now" — Ground truth: rank a named 21-company list for the best commercial AE seat using one consistent framework — exact wedge, ICP, competitive landscape, defensible specific value, and size of the no-brainer customer pool — identical rigor per company so the ranking is head-to-head; deliverable is a top 5 with verified live roles. Source: job-search and edge files, Aug 2026.

7. **Mario Kart** — Input: "whats the best character and kart combo in mario kart 8, what do the good players use" — Ground truth: the current competitive meta build for Mario Kart 8 Deluxe, recency-verified against patch history rather than answered from memory; the actual ask included help writing the research to find it (resolved without a brief because the meta has been frozen since the 2023 balance patch). Source: "Mario Kart 8 competitive meta build" chat, Aug 29 2026.

8. **Food permits** — Input: "i want to sell arepas at events around the bay area, what permits do i need" — Ground truth: the permit path for an event-based Bay Area food concept — MEHKO plus Temporary Food Facility approach, compliance mapped across Alameda, SF, and Santa Clara counties; research-only context, no operating business behind it. Source: food-vending file.

9. **UK visa** — Input: "im a dj and want to move to the uk for good, how hard is that actually" — Ground truth: UK long-term relocation pathways for a professional DJ (noted at the time as for another artist, not the requester's own project); all routes compared — Global Talent (Arts & Culture), Skilled Worker, Creative Worker, self-sponsorship — on eligibility, ILR/citizenship timelines, recent policy changes, and common musician rejection reasons; followed by a step-by-step Global Talent (Exceptional Talent) guide. Source: "UK Visa Pathway" chat, Jan 2026.

10. **Release scanner** — Input: "i keep missing new music i wouldve played in my sets, how do people keep up with releases" — Ground truth: a system that scans releases across a large genre subset and surfaces tracks matching the requester's specific DJ taste; taste fed by uploading a mix or listing songs and artists; output is a regularly updated daily playlist they dig through; architecture open as long as that playlist ships. Source: music-discovery-platform file, Aug 2026.
